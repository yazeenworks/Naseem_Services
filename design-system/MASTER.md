# Al Naseem — design direction

## Direction
Refined minimalism with an editorial hierarchy. Warm ivory surfaces, the existing teal identity, a single dark teal brand section, and an architectural brand sculpture. The selected UI/UX Pro Max Minimalism & Swiss Style result supports RTL fully. Its generic pink recommendation was intentionally replaced with the established teal brand palette.

## Tokens
- Canvas: #FAF9F6
- Body text: #172D2B
- Secondary text: #626C68
- Primary action: #174E46 with white text
- Quiet surface: #E9EFEA
- Dark section: #103E37 with #FAF9F6 / #C3D1C8 text
- Dividers: #D9DED7
- Typeface: DM Sans; expressive accents: DM Serif Display Italic; Arabic: Noto Sans Arabic
- Content width: 1280 px; fluid mobile gutters: 20 px
- Corner radius: 3–5 px; generous section rhythm: 64–108 px

## Interaction
Small scroll reveals, card position transitions, useful native disclosures and a manually controlled process accordion. No autoplay, animated counters, fake customer proof or cursor effects. Reduced-motion removes decorative animation. Motion never hides content by default.

## Content
11 services in 3 groups. Clear English and Arabic routes. Primary action opens WhatsApp; phone and Google Maps use the exact client-provided contact details. All search targeting remains Qatar-based.

## Responsive
Large desktop: split hero, 3-column service grid. Tablet: 2-column service grid. Mobile: visible language switch and collapsed navigation; stacked hero; 2-column services, changing to 1 column at very narrow widths. Arabic uses logical spacing and mirrored directional arrows, preserving the logo and numbers.
